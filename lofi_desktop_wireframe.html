PRINSIP LO-FI Definisi Low-Fidelity Wireframe:

“Low‑fidelity wireframes are simple, rough sketches—usually made with boxes, lines, and placeholder text—that map out a product’s layout, structure, and user flow before any visual design is added. They help teams validate ideas quickly, reduce redesign costs, and focus on UX logic rather than aesthetics.”
